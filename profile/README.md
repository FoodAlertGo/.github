# Food ALERT

**Surplus food should feed people, not landfills.**

Food ALERT connects retail food donors with nonprofits so surplus food is recovered instead of wasted. We build the software that makes a donation, a match, and a pickup happen in hours rather than days.

## How it works

1. **Donors post.** A store lists surplus food, with quantities, temperature needs, allergens, and a pickup window.
2. **We match.** The platform ranks nearby nonprofit sites and offers the donation to the best fit first. A nonprofit accepts or passes, and the next site is offered it.
3. **Drivers collect.** The nonprofit dispatches a driver and vehicle, and pickup proof is recorded.
4. **Everyone stays accountable.** Verified organizations, compliance documents, food-safety certifications, and post-pickup reviews keep recovery safe and reliable.

## What we build

| Piece | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Platform API** | Donations, matching, pickups, organization teams, compliance, messaging, and reporting |
| **Web app** | Donor, nonprofit, and admin consoles |
| **Mobile app** | Donor, nonprofit, driver, and field-operations workflows |

Built with FastAPI, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, Redis, Next.js, and Flutter.

## Who it serves

- **Donors:** grocers and other retailers with surplus food.
- **Nonprofits:** food banks, pantries, and other organizations that distribute it.
- **Field operations teams:** the people who visit stores and check food quality.

Food ALERT serves the United States.
